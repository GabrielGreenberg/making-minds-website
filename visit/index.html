Making Minds

A Constructive Introduction to the Computational Theory of Mind

Gabriel Greenberg
UCLA Department of Philosophy

How does the human mind arise from the unthinking matter of the brain?

According to the Computational Theory of Mind, the brain is a biological computer, and cognition is its function. Making Minds examines this idea by building up, step by step, from the simplest mechanical parts to creatures with cognition, agency, and maybe even consciousness.

The course is taught at UCLA as Philosophy 133. Its materials are open to anyone who wants to follow along.
